Compliance and quality

Importing food into the U.S. comes with rules. Here is how we handle the main ones, so buyers do not have to chase us for answers.

FDA facility registration

Food facilities that make, process, pack, or hold food for U.S. consumption must be registered with the FDA. We confirm this for each supplier we work with.

Foreign supplier verification

As an importer, we keep a written plan to verify that our suppliers meet U.S. food safety expectations, with records of each review.

Prior notice of shipments

We submit advance notice to the FDA before food arrives, so shipments are not held at the port for missing paperwork.

Lot tracking and labeling

Every case carries a lot code that links back to the supplier file, so a problem can be traced and fixed fast.
